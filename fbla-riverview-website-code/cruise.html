Cruises from Tampa — Sail from Port Tampa Bay

A cruise can begin right here, with no drive to Miami. Port Tampa Bay — Florida's largest port and one of the busiest cruise homeports on the Gulf Coast — sails from the Channelside district near downtown Tampa.

🚢 Carnival Cruise Line — Tampa

The largest cruise presence in Tampa, with year-round sailings from Cruise Terminal 2. Family-friendly ships to the Western Caribbean and Mexico — Cozumel, Belize, Roatán, and Grand Cayman.

Source: Port Tampa Bay cruise guides — Carnival, year-round from Tampa.

🚢 Royal Caribbean — Tampa

Western Caribbean and Bahamas sailings from Cruise Terminal 3, including stops at Perfect Day at CocoCay, Royal Caribbean's private island. Midsize ships only here, because of the Sunshine Skyway Bridge.

Source: Port Tampa Bay cruise guides — Royal Caribbean, Terminal 3.

🚢 Norwegian Cruise Line — Tampa

Western Caribbean sailings from Cruise Terminal 6, with freestyle dining — eat when you want, no fixed seating. Norwegian's private island is Great Stirrup Cay in the Bahamas.

Source: Port Tampa Bay cruise guides — Norwegian, Terminal 6.

🚢 Margaritaville at Sea & Celebrity — Tampa

Margaritaville at Sea sails from Terminal 6, and Celebrity Cruises offers premium Caribbean sailings from Terminal 3. MSC Cruises also sails from Tampa seasonally.

Source: Tampa Bay Tourism — 5 lines homeport in Tampa as of 2026.

Port Tampa Bay welcomed a record 1.66 million cruise passengers in 2025 and expects about 1.8 million in 2026. A fourth cruise terminal is being built, due in 2029. Cruise parking is about $15–$20 per night — check before you travel.
