Orlando lies about 84.1 miles from Riverview — roughly 1 hour 38 minutes by car — and ranks among the most visited cities on Earth, with more than 75 million visitors a year. Seeing it all takes 5–7 days, but a single park makes an excellent day trip.

🏰 Walt Disney World — Orlando

The most visited theme park resort on the planet. Four theme parks — Magic Kingdom (with Cinderella Castle), Epcot, Hollywood Studios, and Animal Kingdom — plus two water parks, on 27,000 acres. About $109–$189 per day per park. Book early.

Source: Places to Visit — Walt Disney World, 27,000 acres, 4 parks.

⚡ Universal Orlando Resort — Orlando

Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe, and Volcano Bay water park. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Hagrid's Motorbike Adventure, and the Jurassic World VelociCoaster. About $109–$179 per day. Best for thrill rides.

Source: Places to Visit / Dolphin Family Tours — Universal Orlando, Harry Potter, Epic Universe.

🐬 SeaWorld Orlando — Orlando

Marine animals and roller coasters in one park. Mako — Florida's tallest, fastest, longest roller coaster — plus penguins, dolphins, and shows. About $79–$109 per day. Discovery Cove, next door, lets you swim with dolphins.

Source: Places to Visit — SeaWorld Orlando, Mako coaster.

🦖 Gatorland & ICON Park — Orlando

Gatorland is Old Florida — thousands of alligators and crocodiles, plus a zip line over them. ICON Park on International Drive has The Wheel (a big observation wheel), shops, and restaurants — a good evening stop after the parks.

Source: Dolphin Family Tours — Gatorland, ICON Park, first-timer picks.

Tip: Kennedy Space Center is just 45 minutes east of Orlando — you can add it to an Orlando trip. Orlando is already in our Day Trips table at 84.1 miles.
