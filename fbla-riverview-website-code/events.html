Events & Festivals

Genuine local events with confirmed dates — from a river parade of decorated boats to a community vendors fair. Always verify times through the official links in Sources before travelling, as schedules can change.

🚤 42nd Annual Alafia Lighted Boat Parade

Saturday, Dec. 5, 2026, 4:00–7:00 PM
Riverview Civic Center, 11020 Park Drive, Riverview, FL.

Decorated boats light up the Alafia River, photos with Santa, holiday crafts for kids, a chili cook-off, and live music. This is Riverview's holiday event, Florida style.

🍖 Tampa Bay Vendors Fest

Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, 1:00–8:00 PM
Riverview High School, 11311 Boyette Road, Riverview, FL.

Local vendors, food, a People's Choice BBQ Showdown, live entertainment, and family fun. Admission is free.

🏖️ Pier 60 Sugar Sand Festival — Clearwater Beach

March 27 – April 12, 2026
Clearwater Beach, about 37 miles from Riverview.

Giant sand sculptures, free beach concerts most nights, and fireworks on Saturdays. A spring festival on the Gulf Coast.

  • Winter: Manatee season at Apollo Beach (Nov. 1–April 15) and the Alafia Lighted Boat Parade in December.
  • Spring: Pier 60 Sugar Sand Festival in Clearwater Beach.
  • All year: Farmers markets, school and community events at Riverview Civic Center, and Tampa Riverwalk fun.
