Cinema — Movies in Tampa Bay

From a century-old movie palace to a drive-in operating since 1952, Tampa Bay offers one of Florida's most distinctive cinema scenes — historic glamour and open-air nostalgia in the same weekend.

From a century-old movie palace to a drive-in operating since 1952, Tampa Bay offers one of Florida's most distinctive cinema scenes — historic glamour and open-air nostalgia in the same weekend.

🎬 Tampa Theatre — Downtown Tampa

Built in 1926 — one of America's most elaborate movie palaces, with a starry night-sky ceiling and a Mighty Wurlitzer organ. Its 1,238-seat auditorium hosts nearly 1,000 events a year. Named one of the 10 most beautiful cinemas in the world by the BBC. It turns 100 years old in October 2026.

Source: Tampa Theatre — built 1926, 1,238 seats, ~1,000 events a year.

🎬 AMC West Shore 14 & AMC Veterans 24 — Tampa

The big modern cinemas — AMC West Shore 14 in Westshore Plaza and AMC Veterans 24 in Town 'N Country. New films, big screens, reclining seats, and discount tickets on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Source: Tampa cinema guides — AMC West Shore 14, AMC Veterans 24.

🎬 Ruskin Family Drive-In — Ruskin

A drive-in cinema open since 1952, just south of Riverview in Ruskin. Watch a film from your car, under the stars — the old Florida way to see a movie.

Source: Tampa cinema guides — Ruskin Family Drive-In, since 1952.

🎬 Gasparilla International Film Festival — Tampa

Tampa's big film festival, every March. In 2026 it ran March 3–8, its 19th year — new films, short films, and films from around the world, shown at Tampa Theatre and other venues.

Source: Tampa cinema guides — Gasparilla International Film Festival, March 3–8, 2026.

Also try: CMX CinéBistro in Hyde Park (dinner and a film), Regal Citrus Park, and Green Light Cinema in St. Petersburg (an 80-seat art-house cinema).
