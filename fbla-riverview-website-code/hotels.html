Where to Stay — Good Hotels in Riverview and Tampa

Stay in Riverview itself, or base yourself in Tampa for the Riverwalk and Ybor City. These family-friendly hotels earn consistently high guest ratings — book early, as prices vary considerably by date.

Stay in Riverview itself, or base yourself in Tampa for the Riverwalk and Ybor City. These family-friendly hotels earn consistently high guest ratings — book early, as prices vary considerably by date.

🏨 Fairfield Inn & Suites Tampa Riverview — Riverview

Rated 9.4 out of 10 by guests — the top-rated family hotel in Riverview. Clean rooms, comfortable beds, a pool, free breakfast, free cribs, and rollaway beds for families.

Source: Expedia — Fairfield Inn & Suites Tampa Riverview, 9.4 rating.

🏨 Hilton Garden Inn Tampa / Riverview / Brandon — Riverview

Rated 4.4 out of 5, the #1 Best Value family hotel in Riverview. Breakfast included, a pool, and easy drives to Tampa and Brandon.

Source: TripAdvisor — Hilton Garden Inn Tampa/Riverview/Brandon, 4.4 stars.

🏨 Home2 Suites by Hilton Riverview Tampa — Riverview

Suites with a kitchen — good for a longer stay or a family that wants to cook. In Riverview, close to shops and restaurants.

Source: Trivago — Home2 Suites by Hilton Riverview Tampa.

🏨 Embassy Suites by Hilton Tampa Brandon — Brandon

About 7.8 km from Riverview. Big suites, free breakfast, and a pool — a good pick for families who want more room.

Source: TripAdvisor — Embassy Suites Tampa Brandon, 7.8 km from Riverview.

🏨 The Barrymore Hotel Tampa Riverwalk — Tampa

About 10 miles from Riverview, rated 8.6 out of 10. On the Tampa Riverwalk — walk to the Florida Aquarium, museums, and restaurants. Pet-friendly.

Source: Expedia — The Barrymore Hotel Tampa Riverwalk, 8.6 rating, 10 miles.

🏨 Tampa Marriott Water Street — Tampa

A big family-friendly hotel on Tampa's Water Street, near the Riverwalk, the Aquarium, and Amalie Arena. Good if you want to stay right in downtown Tampa.

Source: Trivago — Tampa Marriott Water Street, family-friendly.

Prices change by date and season — check the hotel's own site or a booking site before you travel. Riverview hotels are small and fill up fast on event weekends, so book early.
