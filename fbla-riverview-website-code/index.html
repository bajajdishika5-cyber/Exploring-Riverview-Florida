Explore Riverview, Florida

Tampa Bay's historic river town — where the Alafia River winds past nature preserves, citrus groves, and more than a century of Florida stories.

Riverview, Florida — our hometown in Hillsborough County, on the Alafia River just south of Tampa — is the focus of this guide. Written for first-time visitors, it introduces Riverview and Tampa, then looks outward to day trips within 200 miles, with every fact traced to a real source.

Welcome to Riverview, Florida

What is this website about? It is a visitors bureau website for our hometown, Riverview, Florida — a sunny, family-friendly town along the Alafia River in southeastern Hillsborough County, close to Tampa Bay. Riverview combines nature preserves, river recreation, and local history with easy access to Tampa, Gulf beaches, and Orlando, and this site brings all of it together in one place.

Who is it for? A family visiting for the first time, a friend from another state, or a local resident looking for fresh weekend ideas. Every recommendation names a real place, a real date, and a real source, so visitors can plan with confidence instead of guessing.
