Beautiful Houses to See — Historic Mansions

These are no ordinary houses. Each is a historic mansion open for tours, with gardens, art, and architecture that preserve chapters of Florida's past — and all lie within 200 miles of Riverview.

These are no ordinary houses. Each is a historic mansion open for tours, with gardens, art, and architecture that preserve chapters of Florida's past — and all lie within 200 miles of Riverview.

🏰 Henry B. Plant Museum — Tampa

The old Tampa Bay Hotel, built in 1891 for $2.5 million. Moorish Revival style with silver minarets and onion domes — the most famous building in Tampa. Now a museum and part of the University of Tampa, at 401 W. Kennedy Blvd.

Source: Engineers and Architects of America — Tampa Bay Hotel, built 1891, $2.5M.

🏰 Ca' d'Zan — Sarasota (~60 miles)

The winter home of circus king John Ringling, built 1924–1926. Venetian Gothic style, 56 rooms and 36,000 square feet, on Sarasota Bay. "Ca' d'Zan" means "House of John". Part of The Ringling museum estate.

Source: EAA / Its My Nest — Ca' d'Zan, 1924–1926, 56 rooms, 36,000 sq ft.

🏡 Edison and Ford Winter Estates — Fort Myers (~130 miles)

The winter homes of Thomas Edison and Henry Ford, next door to each other. Edison bought land in 1885 and finished his home in 1886; Ford bought the house next door in 1916. Over 20 acres of homes, gardens, a museum, and Edison's lab, with a famous giant banyan tree.

Source: Edison & Ford Winter Estates / TravelMag — 20+ acres, Edison 1885, Ford 1916.

🏛️ Gamble Plantation — Ellenton

A grand pillared Greek Revival mansion, built 1844–1856. It has 10 rooms and was once the centre of a 3,450-acre sugar plantation. It is the last surviving antebellum plantation home in South Florida, and you can walk its grounds.

Source: MustDo — Gamble Plantation, 1844–1856, 10 rooms, 3,450 acres.

Tip: these houses are museums — check opening hours and ticket prices before you go, and take photos outside where allowed.
