Explore Riverview, Florida

Attractions in Riverview

These are the defining places in Riverview itself — the heart of our local area, from a one-of-a-kind carnival museum to thousands of acres of protected Florida wilderness.

These are the defining places in Riverview itself — the heart of our local area, from a one-of-a-kind carnival museum to thousands of acres of protected Florida wilderness.

🎪 International Independent Showmen's Museum

Our most special place. This museum tells the history of the American carnival and circus. It was established in 2013. You can see colorful costumes, rides, and carnival history you will not find in most towns.

Source: Wondrous Drifter / World Kids Travel — Riverview guide.

🌿 Alafia Scrub Nature Preserve

80 acres of hiking and biking trails with views of the Alafia River. A quiet place to see birds, gopher tortoises, and wild Florida plants. Best for birdwatching from November to May.

Source: World Kids Travel — Alafia Scrub, 80 acres.

🌳 Bell Creek Nature Preserve

477 acres for hiking and birdwatching. A big, wild park close to home, with trails through Florida nature.

Source: World Kids Travel — Bell Creek, 477 acres.

🥾 Triple Creek Nature Preserve

1,200 acres with miles of hiking trails. This is the quiet, wild side of Riverview — great for a nature walk with family.

Source: Wondrous Drifter — Triple Creek, 1,200 acres.

🎳 The Alley at Southshore

Bowling, laser tag, billiards, and an arcade. This fun center was established in 2008. Perfect for a rainy day or a birthday with friends.

Source: World Kids Travel — The Alley at Southshore, established 2008.

⛳ Ace Golf & Riverview Civic Center

Ace Golf: mini-golf and a driving range. Riverview Civic Center: a 9-acre park with a playground, picnic spots, and a place to launch kayaks on the Alafia River.

Source: Wondrous Drifter / Komoot — Riverview Civic Center kayaking.

Attractions in Tampa — Nearby

Tampa is our big nearby city, about 20–30 minutes from Riverview. These are the top Tampa places for visitors.

🎢 Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

A 335-acre African-themed park with roller coasters and one of North America's largest zoos. A full day of rides and animals.

Source: Travel.com — Busch Gardens, 335 acres.

🐠 The Florida Aquarium

Home to more than 8,000 aquatic plants and animals, from Florida springs to the deep sea. Touch tanks and fun for all ages, on the Tampa Riverwalk.

Source: Travel.com — Florida Aquarium, 8,000+ animals.

🦁 ZooTampa at Lowry Park

More than 1,300 animals across 56 acres. One of the top family zoos in the country, with manatee care, elephants, tigers, and a splash pad.

Source: Travel.com — ZooTampa, 1,300 animals, 56 acres.

🚶 Tampa Riverwalk

A 2.6-mile walk along the Hillsborough River. It connects the Florida Aquarium, Tampa Museum of Art, parks, food, and Amalie Arena. Free to walk, day or night.

Source: Travel.com — Tampa Riverwalk, 2.6 miles.

🏛️ Ybor City

Tampa's historic Latin Quarter, with Cuban, Spanish, and Italian history. Walk 7th Avenue, see old cigar shops, and try Cuban food at the Columbia Restaurant.

Source: Visit Tampa Bay / Tampa guides — Ybor City historic district.

🐬 TECO Manatee Viewing Center — Apollo Beach

Free to visit. Manatees gather in the warm water by the Big Bend Power Station in winter. Open Nov. 1, 2026 – April 15, 2027, 10 AM–5 PM. At 6990 Dickman Road, Apollo Beach. On one cold day last season, a record 2,100 manatees were counted here.

Source: Tampa Bay Observer / Tampa Electric — 40th season, free, Nov. 1–April 15.
