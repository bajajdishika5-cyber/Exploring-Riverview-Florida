Big Companies and Campuses — Who Works Here

Riverview and Tampa Bay are far more than beaches and theme parks. Global headquarters, Florida's largest port, and a major military base all operate here — and understanding that working economy is part of understanding the area.

🏦 Citi — Tampa Campus

Citi's global operational headquarters is in Tampa. A 670,000-square-foot campus with 5 buildings, created in 1997. Citi employs about 10,000 people in the Tampa region — its second-largest office in the USA. You cannot tour it, but it is a big part of why Tampa grows.

Source: Tradeline / EZ Home Search — Citi Tampa campus, 670,000 sq ft, ~10,000 people.

💼 Raymond James — St. Petersburg

Headquarters in St. Petersburg, founded in 1962. A big finance company with $13.0 billion in revenue in 2024. Its stadium — Raymond James Stadium — is where the Tampa Bay Buccaneers play.

Source: Tampa Bay Business News — Raymond James, founded 1962, $13.0B.

🏭 Jabil — St. Petersburg

Headquarters in St. Petersburg for over 30 years. A huge maker of electronics with $34.7 billion in revenue and 100 plants in 30 countries.

Source: Tampa Bay Business News — Jabil, $34.7B, 100 plants.

💻 TD Synnex — Clearwater / Largo

Tampa Bay's largest public company. A technology company formed in 2021 from Tech Data and Synnex. It sells computers and tech to businesses all over the world.

Source: Tampa Bay Business News — TD Synnex, Tampa Bay's largest public company.

🌱 The Mosaic Company — Tampa

Headquarters in Tampa, on East Kennedy Boulevard. A fertiliser and phosphate company with $13.7 billion in revenue in 2024 and a Fortune 500 rank of #307.

Source: Tampa Bay Business News — Mosaic, Tampa, $13.7B, Fortune 500 #307.

✈️ MacDill Air Force Base — Tampa

About 4 miles south of downtown Tampa. Home to the headquarters of US Central Command and US Special Operations Command. About 15,000 people work there, and it adds over $3 billion to our local economy.

Source: Wikipedia — MacDill AFB, 4 miles from downtown, 15,000 people.

🚢 Port Tampa Bay — Tampa

Florida's largest port, over 5,000 acres. It handles cargo, cruise ships, and cars, with an economic impact of over $15.1 billion and over 80,000 jobs supported.

Source: Wikipedia — Port Tampa Bay, 5,000+ acres, $15.1B, 80,000+ jobs.

📦 Amazon — Ruskin (near Riverview)

Amazon's first Florida fulfilment centre is in Ruskin, just south of Riverview, with over 3,000 employees. Tampa Bay is one of Amazon's biggest markets in Florida.

Source: Tampa Bay EDC — Amazon, first Florida fulfilment centre in Ruskin, 3,000+ jobs.

Also headquartered in Tampa Bay: Bloomin' Brands (Outback Steakhouse), TECO Energy, and Suncoast Credit Union — Florida's largest credit union.
