Where to Eat — Good Food in Riverview and Tampa

Come hungry. These are established, family-friendly restaurants that locals recommend — from a Southern buffet in Riverview to Florida's oldest restaurant, serving Spanish-Cuban food in Tampa since 1905.

Come hungry. These are established, family-friendly restaurants that locals recommend — from a Southern buffet in Riverview to Florida's oldest restaurant, serving Spanish-Cuban food in Tampa since 1905.

🍗 Fred's Market Restaurant — Riverview

Southern comfort buffet at 6501 US-301. Fried chicken, biscuits, and home-style sides. Rated 4.3 out of 5 by visitors. Best for a big family brunch.

Source: TripAdvisor — Fred's Market, 4.3 stars.

🥞 Keke's Breakfast Cafe — Riverview

Huge pancakes, waffles, and stuffed French toast at 11430 US-301. A weekend brunch favourite in Riverview — come early, it fills up fast.

Source: Tampa Bay brunch guide — Keke's, Riverview.

🍜 Tuk Tuk Thai Fusion — Riverview

Thai food, rated 4.7 out of 5. Noodles, curries, and vegetarian-friendly dishes — a tasty change from burgers and pizza.

Source: DoorDash / TripAdvisor — Tuk Tuk Thai Fusion, 4.7 stars.

🥩 Donovan's Meatery — Riverview

Wood-fired steaks and prime rib. A local favourite for a special dinner, with rich flavour from real wood fire.

Source: AutoReserve — Donovan's Meatery, wood-fired cooking.

🇨🇺 Columbia Restaurant — Ybor City, Tampa

Florida's oldest restaurant, opened in 1905. Spanish-Cuban food, flamenco shows on weekends, and the famous 1905 Salad made right at your table.

Source: Feastio / Tampa food guides — Columbia, opened 1905.

🐟 Ulele — Tampa Riverwalk

Fresh Gulf oysters and blackened grouper on the Riverwalk, in a restored 1903 water station. Native-inspired food from Florida's waters and lands.

Source: McCool Travel — Ulele, Riverwalk, Tampa.

🥪 La Teresita — Tampa

A real Cuban diner, open nearly 24/7. Cheap, fast, and real — try a Cuban sandwich and a café con leche.

Source: Tampa Pulse — La Teresita, Cuban diner.

🥩 Bern's Steak House — Tampa

One of the best steakhouses in the USA, with a whole room just for dessert and one of the largest wine collections in the world. A special-occasion treat.

Source: McCool Travel — Bern's Steak House, Tampa.

Must-try foods in our area

  • Tampa Cuban sandwich — Cuban bread, roast pork, ham, salami, Swiss cheese, pickles, and mustard, pressed hot. Try it in Ybor City.
  • Blackened grouper — fresh Gulf fish, best at Ulele on the Riverwalk.
  • Stone crab claws — in season October to May, served cold with mustard sauce.
  • 1905 Salad — made at your table at Columbia Restaurant, Ybor City.
