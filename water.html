Water defines life here: the Alafia River, Tampa Bay, and the Gulf of Mexico are all within easy reach. Here is how to experience them — on the surface, beneath it, and along the shore. Always wear a life jacket, and check the weather first.

🛶 Kayaking the Alafia River — Riverview

Kayak or canoe right here in Riverview, on the Alafia River. Alderman's Ford Park, up the river, has a sandy bottom and trees overhead. You may see turtles, ospreys, and manatees. Rentals are available in Riverview.

Source: Bill Jackson's — Alderman's Ford, Alafia River kayaking.

🏄 Paddleboarding the Tampa Riverwalk — Tampa

Stand-up paddleboarding on the Hillsborough River, past downtown Tampa's skyline. Calm water, good for beginners — and you may see dolphins and manatees. Rent a board on the Riverwalk.

Source: Quartz Mountain — paddleboarding, Tampa Riverwalk.

🐬 Manatee Watching — Apollo Beach

See manatees for free at the TECO Manatee Viewing Center, Apollo Beach, Nov 1 to Apr 15. A record 2,100 manatees were counted there on one cold day. You can also kayak with manatees at Crystal River (~84 miles) and Three Sisters Springs.

Source: TECO Manatee Viewing Center — free, Nov 1–Apr 15, record 2,100.

🏖️ Clearwater Beach & Fort De Soto — Day Trips

Clearwater Beach (36.7 miles) — soft white sand, jet skiing, and parasailing. Fort De Soto Park — 1,136 acres, kayaking to Shell Key Preserve, and clear water for snorkelling at Egmont Key.

Source: Quartz Mountain / Bill Jackson's — Clearwater Beach, Fort De Soto, Shell Key.

✨ Glow Water Kayaking — Cocoa Beach (~140 miles)

Bioluminescent kayaking — at night, the water glows blue-green around your paddle, May to early November. Best near Cocoa Beach and Merritt Island, on the Space Coast.

Source: Riverview Secret Map research — bioluminescence, May–early Nov.

🤿 Snorkelling & Swimming with Manatees — Crystal River

Swim with manatees at Crystal River (~84 miles), in clear spring water at Three Sisters Springs. You can also snorkel at Egmont Key, and swim at Weeki Wachee Springs (~58 miles), where mermaids perform underwater.

Source: Secret Map / Day Trips — Crystal River, Weeki Wachee.

Safety: never touch a manatee — watch from a distance. Wear sunscreen, drink water, and check for red tide and weather warnings before you go in the Gulf.
