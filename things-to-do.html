Explore Riverview, Florida

Things to Do

What sets Riverview apart is variety: in a single weekend, visitors can paddle a river, hike a thousand-acre preserve, and end the evening at a family fun centre — all without leaving the area.

What sets Riverview apart is variety: in a single weekend, visitors can paddle a river, hike a thousand-acre preserve, and end the evening at a family fun centre — all without leaving the area.

🛶 Kayak the Alafia River

Paddle or paddleboard from Riverview Civic Center, watching for herons, ospreys, and fish beneath the surface. Bring water, sunscreen, and a life jacket.

🥾 Hike a Preserve

Alafia Scrub (80 acres), Bell Creek (477 acres), and Triple Creek (1,200 acres) offer trails ranging from gentle walks to demanding full-day hikes through wild Florida.

🎳 Family Fun Night

Bowling, laser tag, and arcade games at The Alley at Southshore, or mini-golf and a driving range at Ace Golf — easy evening fun for the whole family.

🎣 Fish Tampa Bay

Fish from the shore, a pier, or a charter boat into Tampa Bay. Local bait shops can tell you exactly what is biting this week.

🍽️ Try Local Food

Breakfast at The Egg & I, Mexican food at La Villa Mexican Grill, fresh seafood at The Fish House, and authentic Cuban food in Tampa's Ybor City.

🛍️ Shop Local

Winthrop Town Centre in Riverview for shops and restaurants, plus the Riverview Arts Market and local farmers markets for handmade goods and fresh produce.
