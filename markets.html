Markets to Shop — Good Places to Buy Things

Shopping reveals a place's character: Riverview's walkable town centre, Brandon's major mall, and the weekend farmers markets each show a different side of the area. Here is where to find them.

Shopping reveals a place's character: Riverview's walkable town centre, Brandon's major mall, and the weekend farmers markets each show a different side of the area. Here is where to find them.

🛍️ Winthrop Town Centre — Riverview

Riverview's walkable town centre — unique shops, boutiques, a Publix, and restaurants on a main street. It also hosts a farmers market every Saturday and a Pop Up Market with 150+ local vendors on the third Saturday of the month, October to May.

Source: Highland Homes / The New Place — Winthrop Town Centre, Riverview.

🛒 Westfield Brandon Mall — Brandon

Over 140 specialty and department stores, close to Riverview. The biggest mall in our area — clothes, gifts, food, and a movie theatre, all in one place.

Source: Highland Homes — Westfield Brandon Mall, 140+ stores.

🎪 The Alley at SouthShore — Riverview

Est. 2008 — Riverview's fun shopping and dining spot, with shops, food, and things to do for families. A good budget-friendly stop.

Source: Riverview shopping guides — The Alley at SouthShore, est. 2008.

🥕 Brandon Sunday Market — Brandon

Sundays, 9 AM to 1 PM, at 788 W. Lumsden Road, Brandon. Now in its 10th year — local food, crafts, and handmade goods from local sellers.

Source: Brandon Sunday Market — Sundays 9 AM–1 PM.

🏷️ Tampa Premium Outlets — Tampa

Outlet shopping — big brands for less money. A good stop for a day trip to Tampa, especially if you want clothes or shoes.

Source: Riverview shopping guides — Tampa Premium Outlets.

🛒 Riverview Flea Market — Riverview

A weekend market with vintage goods, antiques, handmade crafts, and fresh local produce. Great for bargain hunters and for finding something no one else has.

Source: Riverview shopping guides — Riverview Flea Market.

Groceries, if you are staying a while

  • Publix Super Market — Florida's favourite grocery store, in Winthrop Town Centre and all over Riverview.
  • Sprouts Farmers Market — fresh and healthy food, on the south side of Riverview.
  • ALDI — a low-cost grocery store, also in Riverview.
