Explore Riverview, Florida

Plan Your Visit

When to go, how to get here, and three ready-made day plans.

Best Time to Visit

  • November–April: Best for manatees at Apollo Beach and cool, sunny weather. Manatee Center is open Nov. 1–April 15.
  • November–May: Best for birdwatching at Alafia Scrub Nature Preserve.
  • December: Alafia Lighted Boat Parade.
  • Summer: Hot and sunny — great for beaches, water parks, and early-morning hikes. Drink water and wear sunscreen.

How to Get Here

  • By car: Riverview is on U.S. Highway 301 and near Interstate 75, south of Tampa.
  • By plane: Fly to Tampa International Airport (TPA), then drive about 30 minutes to Riverview.
  • Around town: A car is the easiest way to visit preserves, Tampa, and beaches.

Sample Plans

1-Day Plan — Riverview

Morning: Kayak the Alafia River. Afternoon: Showmen's Museum + Alafia Scrub hike. Evening: Dinner in Riverview and a walk at Winthrop Town Centre.

2-Day Plan — Riverview + Tampa

Day 1: Riverview as above. Day 2: Tampa Riverwalk, Florida Aquarium, and Ybor City for dinner.

3-Day Plan — Add a Beach or Orlando

Day 3: Clearwater Beach and Pier 60 sunset — OR — Orlando for Walt Disney World or Universal.

What to pack: Sunscreen, a water bottle, a hat, comfortable walking shoes, insect repellent for the trails, and a camera.
