Tampa Bay is a major centre of higher education — about 80,000 students study in Tampa alone, across 18 colleges. The universities below are the largest in and around our area, all within 200 miles of Riverview, and each shapes the region's culture and economy.

🎓 University of South Florida (USF) — Tampa

Nearly 50,000 students, founded in 1956. The main Tampa campus is 1,562 acres with over 41,000 students and 14 colleges. Famous for health, cybersecurity, and biomedical engineering.

Source: USF / Wikipedia — USF, nearly 50,000 students, Tampa campus 1,562 acres.

🎓 University of Tampa — Tampa

About 11,400 students, a private university in downtown Tampa. Its home is the beautiful Henry B. Plant Museum building (the old Tampa Bay Hotel, 1891), with silver minarets you can see across the city.

Source: UniHub — University of Tampa, 11,433 students.

🎓 University of Central Florida (UCF) — Orlando

About 69,700 students — the largest university in Florida and one of the largest in the USA. In Orlando, about 84 miles from Riverview. Famous for engineering, space research, and hospitality.

Source: UniHub — UCF, Orlando, 69,713 students.

🎓 University of Florida (UF) — Gainesville

About 56,300 students, ranked #1 among public universities in Florida and #5 in the whole USA. In Gainesville, within 200 miles of Riverview. Famous for engineering, business, and pre-med.

Source: UniHub — UF, Gainesville, 56,311 students.

Also nearby: Hillsborough Community College (in Tampa and Riverview), Saint Leo University, Eckerd College in St. Petersburg, and Florida Polytechnic University in Lakeland.
