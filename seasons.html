Seasonal Activities — Pick, Taste, and Celebrate All Year

Florida's harvest calendar rarely pauses: while much of the country waits for summer, our farms pick strawberries in January, blueberries in April, and citrus all winter long. Around Riverview, within 200 miles, visitors can pick their own fruit, meet the farmers who grow it, and celebrate the harvest at two of the largest fairs in the Southeast. Farm seasons depend on weather and crops, so always check the farm's own website or call ahead before you drive over.

🍓 Strawberries — Plant City, Dec–Mar

Plant City is the Winter Strawberry Capital of the World. At Parkesdale Farm Market (3702 W Baker St), Florida's largest strawberry and citrus market, family-run since 1969, try the famous strawberry shortcake (about $7) — open daily 9 AM–6 PM, December to early April. At Keel Farms (5202 Thonotosassa Rd), pick your own strawberries January–March, fields open daily 11 AM to sundown, $5 for a small bucket and $10 for a large one.

Source: Parkesdale Farm Market and Keel Farms — U-Pick

🍊 Oranges & Honeybells — Ruskin, Nov–Apr

Pick citrus straight from the tree at Dooley Groves (1651 Stephens Rd, Ruskin, about 12 miles from Riverview) — Florida's largest U-pick Honeybell grove, with more than 6,500 trees. U-pick oranges and tangerines run November–April, 10 AM–4 PM, with Honeybells — the sweetest citrus many visitors ever taste — in January and February. Family-run for three generations, with free samples and freshly squeezed orange juice.

Source: Dooley Groves

🫐 Blueberries — Plant City & Lithia, Apr–May

Blueberry season is short — April to May — so time it well. Keel Farms in Plant City fills buckets with sweet blueberries in April and May. Wish Farms (1301 Frontage Rd S, Plant City) opens its blueberry fields on select days in April and May. Closest to home, Lyna Berry Farms (11920 Neal Rd, Lithia, about 10 miles from Riverview) offers U-pick blueberries from mid-April through May at $3.75 a pound.

Source: Keel Farms — Fruit Picking Seasons and Tampa Bay Date Night Guide — U-Pick Farms

🍑 Peaches — Clermont, Apr–May

Southern Hill Farms (16651 Schofield Rd, Clermont, about 95 miles from Riverview) is a 120-acre family farm with 5 acres of peach trees and 40 acres of blueberry bushes. Pick peaches in April and May, blueberries in March–May, and strawberries in December–March — plus sunflowers in spring and fall. Each picking area has its own greeter to explain the rules.

Source: Orlando Parenting — U-Pick Farms

🎃 Pumpkins & Fall Fun — Bradenton & Clermont, Sept–Oct

Hunsader Farms (5500 CR 675, Bradenton, about 45 miles from Riverview), founded in 1967 and open September to early June, runs a pumpkin patch and fall festival with a petting zoo, playground, and farmers market. Southern Hill Farms in Clermont holds its own Fall Festival in October, with pumpkins, sunflowers, and family activities across its 120 acres.

Source: Orlando Parenting — U-Pick Farms

🎡 State Fairs — Tampa & Plant City, February

February is fair month. The Florida State Fair runs February 5–16, 2026, at the Florida State Fairgrounds (4800 US Hwy 301 N, Tampa) — its 122nd year, with 90+ rides, farm animals, live shows, and fair food, celebrating America's 250th birthday. Then the Florida Strawberry Festival runs February 26–March 8, 2026, in Plant City — 11 days, more than 500,000 visitors, concerts, rides, and strawberry shortcake everywhere.

Source: Theme Park Hipster — Florida State Fair 2026 and Florida Marvels — Strawberry Festival 2026
